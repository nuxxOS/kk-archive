Outworld / Track Themes

Space-native directions pulled from his actual catalog — Dimension Null, Man on the Moon, Sonne Geht Auf, Rise of the Phoenix, Toter Schmetterling. Same rule as above: original glyphs, nothing traced from Outworld's spike mark or any cover art. Each card ends with a prompt for your design AI.

I — Null Planet (Dimension Null)

The null symbol ∅ read as a ringed planet: one circle, one orbit line striking through. "Dimension Null" and generic-space in a single stroke — the most reducible mark on this page, survives any size.
